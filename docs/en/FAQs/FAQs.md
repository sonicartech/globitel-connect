## General { #general }

???+ question "What is Globitel Connect?"
    Globitel Connect is a video customer-engagement platform. Customers can book an appointment or ask for immediate support, then talk face to face with an agent by video, chat and file sharing.

    It has two portals:

    - **Agent & Supervisor Portal**: handle customer calls and monitor your team. [Get started](../Getting-started/Login.md)
    - **Admin Portal**: manage users, teams, skills, routing rules and system settings. [Admin guide](../Administration/log-in.md)

??? question "How do I open Globitel Connect?"
    1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome is recommended).
    2. Enter the address your administrator gave you, in the format `http://Server_Name:Port/gc-mobile`.
    3. Sign in with your **Username** and **Password**.

    [Read the full guide](../Getting-started/Login.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "Which browser should I use?"
    We recommend **Google Chrome** on Windows, macOS or Linux. [Download Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/).

??? question "How do I change the portal language?"
    Click the drop-down menu next to your profile picture in the header and choose **English** or **العربية**.

??? question "How do I change my password?"
    1. Click the drop-down menu next to your profile picture in the header.
    2. Choose **Change Password**.
    3. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm the new one, and click **Save**.

    !!! tip "Tip"
        Use at least eight characters, including a number, a special character, an uppercase letter and a lowercase letter.

    [Read the full guide](../My-profile/Change-password.md){ .gc-faq-more }

## Agents { #agents }

??? question "Do I need a camera and a microphone?"
    Yes. Your computer needs a working camera and microphone (built in or external). Without them you can't receive customer calls.

    [Test your camera and microphone](../Getting-started/Testing-your-camera-and-microphone.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "Why do I have to share my entire screen?"
    Screen sharing is required for **quality monitoring**: the system records your screen activity while you work. You can't open your dashboard or handle calls until you share your entire screen.

    When the pop-up appears after you log in:

    1. Choose the **Entire Screen** tab.
    2. Select your screen.
    3. Click **Share**.

    [Read the full guide](../Getting-started/Share-screen.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "How do I start receiving calls?"
    Click the **Ready** button under your profile panel on the dashboard. Until you're Ready, the system treats you as busy and won't send you any calls.

    [Read the full guide](../Getting-started/Getting-ready-for-engagements.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "I'm not receiving any calls. What should I check?"
    Go through this checklist:

    - [x] Your status is **Ready**. Missing a call sets you back to **Not Ready**, so press **Ready** again.
    - [x] You shared your **entire screen** after logging in.
    - [x] Your camera and microphone work. [Run the test](../Getting-started/Testing-your-camera-and-microphone.md).
    - [x] You're using an up-to-date version of Google Chrome.

    If everything looks right and calls still don't arrive, contact your supervisor or administrator.

??? question "What happens if I miss or reject a call?"
    - **Missed call**: if you don't answer within **5 seconds**, the call goes to another ready agent and your status changes to **Not Ready**.
    - **Rejected call**: the call goes back to the queue and the rejection is recorded. Only reject calls in an emergency.

    [Read the full guide](../Handling-Interactions/Receiving-a-new-interaction.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "What can I do during a call?"
    From the meeting room you can:

    - [Chat with the customer](../Handling-Interactions/Chat.md)
    - [Share files](../Handling-Interactions/Exchanging-documents.md)
    - [Share your screen](../Handling-Interactions/Screen-sharing.md)
    - [Change or blur your background](../Handling-Interactions/Change-background-and-blur.md)
    - [Transfer the call to another agent](../Handling-Interactions/Call-Transfer.md)
    - [Invite others into a conference](../Handling-Interactions/Conference-room-functions.md)

??? question "How do I send a file to the customer?"
    1. Click **Chat** in the meeting room.
    2. Click the **File** icon and choose your file.
    3. Press **Enter** to send it.

    You can send images, videos, documents (PDF, DOCX, XLSX) and ZIP files.

    !!! warning "Caution"
        Download any important files before the call ends. Files in the chat are lost when the call finishes.

    [Read the full guide](../Handling-Interactions/Exchanging-documents.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "How do I update my profile photo?"
    In the profile section of your dashboard, click the **camera icon** next to your photo and take a new picture. Customers see this photo during calls.

    [Read the full guide](../My-profile/Modify-profile-photo.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "Where can I see my upcoming appointments?"
    Open the **Appointments** tab on your dashboard. It shows each appointment's time and status: upcoming, rescheduled, completed or cancelled.

    [Read the full guide](../Getting-started/Appointments.md){ .gc-faq-more }

## Customers { #customers }

??? question "How do I request a meeting with an agent?"
    1. Open the link provided by customer support.
    2. Fill in your details.
    3. Choose a meeting type:
        - **Appointment**: pick a date and time that suits you.
        - **Immediate Support**: talk to an agent now.
    4. Click **Book Appointment**. A pop-up shows your meeting link.

    [Read the full guide](../Customer-Access/Requesting-a-Meeting.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "Will I get the meeting link by email?"
    Yes. After booking, you receive an email with your meeting link, the date and time, and your details.

??? question "How do I join my meeting?"
    1. Open your meeting link in the browser.
    2. Unmute your microphone and turn on your camera.
    3. Click **Ready for Engagements** to start talking with the agent.

    [Read the full guide](../Customer-Access/Starting-a-Meeting.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "What can I do during the meeting?"
    You can turn your camera on or off, mute your microphone, [share your screen](../Handling-Interactions/Screen-sharing.md), [change your background](../Handling-Interactions/Change-background-and-blur.md), [chat](../Handling-Interactions/Chat.md) and [exchange files](../Handling-Interactions/Exchanging-documents.md) with the agent.

    When you hang up, you can rate your experience from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

## Supervisors { #supervisors }

??? question "How can I monitor my team?"
    Use the **Real Time Monitor** to:

    - [Track each agent's performance](../Real-Time-Monitor/Track-agents-performance.md): calls handled, average handling time and customer satisfaction.
    - [See live team status and appointments](../Real-Time-Monitor/Monitoring-current-interactions.md), filtered by today, this week, this month or last month.

??? question "Can I listen to or join an agent's live call?"
    ![Interaction actions](../assets/Real-Time-Monitor/Actions.jpg "Interaction actions"){ width=65, align=right }
    Yes. In the **Team Status** tab, click the options icon (:material-dots-horizontal-circle:) next to an active call:

    - **Listen**: hear the call silently.
    - **Whisper**: coach the agent; only the agent hears you.
    - **Barge In**: join the call as a three-way conversation.

    [Read the full guide](../Real-Time-Monitor/Monitoring-current-interactions.md){ .gc-faq-more }

??? question "How do I cancel a customer's appointment?"
    ![Appointment actions](../assets/Real-Time-Monitor/Appointments_Actions.jpg "Appointment actions"){ width=65, align=right }
    Open the **Team Appointments** tab, click the options icon (:material-dots-horizontal-circle:) next to the appointment, choose **Cancel** and confirm.

??? question "Can I export my team's appointments?"
    Yes. In **Team Appointments**, select a start and end date, then click **Export** to download an Excel file.

??? question "Do supervisors need to set themselves to Ready?"
    Yes, if you handle calls transferred from your team. Otherwise the system treats you as busy and won't send you transferred calls.

    1. Go to **User Management** > **Users**.
    2. Click the add icon (:octicons-diff-added-16:).
    3. Fill in the user's details and choose their group, team and skills.
    4. Click **Save**.

    [Read the full guide](../Administration/User-Management/Users-Management.md){ .gc-faq-more }

    Open **About System** from the side menu to see the product version and the maximum number of agents your license allows.
